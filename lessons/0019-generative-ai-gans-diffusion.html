Generative AI: GANs & Diffusion Models 🎨

Level: Advanced Topic: Generative vs Discriminative, GAN Minimax Game, Latent Space & Diffusion ← Lesson 0018

1. WHY Move Beyond Discriminative Models? (Drawbacks of Only Predicting Labels $P(y|x)$)

  • Discriminative AI: Learns $P(y|x)$ to draw boundaries between categories (e.g. "Is this a cat or a dog?").
  • Generative AI: Learns the underlying data distribution $P(x)$ to create brand-new synthetic data from scratch!
🌍 Real-World Industry Context & Case Studies

Generative AI moves from predicting labels $P(y|x)$ to creating brand new content $P(x)$:

  • 🎨 Text-to-Image Generation (Midjourney / Stable Diffusion): Generating high-resolution digital art, marketing imagery, and photorealistic concept designs from text prompts.
  • 💊 Drug Discovery & Molecular Design: Generative AI designs novel protein structures and drug candidate molecules in latent chemical spaces.
  • 🧪 Synthetic Data Generation: Generating privacy-compliant synthetic medical patient data or rare autonomous driving corner-case simulation frames.

2. WHAT IS IT? (Generative Modeling $P(x)$ with GANs, VAEs, and Diffusion)

Pioneered by Ian Goodfellow (2014), a GAN consists of two neural networks locked in a game of cat-and-mouse:

The Counterfeiter vs Cop Game
  • Generator $G(z)$: The Counterfeiter. Takes random noise $z$ and tries to produce fake images realistic enough to trick the cop.
  • Discriminator $D(x)$: The Cop. Takes real images and fake generated images and tries to classify them as 1 (Real) or 0 (Fake).

The GAN Minimax Objective Function:

\[ \min_G \max_D V(D, G) = \mathbb{E}_{x \sim p_{\text{data}}} [\log D(x)] + \mathbb{E}_{z \sim p_z} [\log (1 - D(G(z)))] \]

3. HOW Does It Work? (GAN Minimax Counterfeiter vs Cop Game & Diffusion Denoising U-Net)

Why have Diffusion Models surpassed GANs in image quality?

  1. Forward Process (Noise Addition): Slowly add Gaussian noise to a clean image over $T=1000$ steps until it becomes pure static noise.
  2. Reverse Process (Denoising): Train a U-Net neural network to predict and remove noise step-by-step, transforming pure static back into a crystal-clear image!
🎨 Mode Collapse in GANs & Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG)
  • Mode Collapse in GANs: The generator discovers a single realistic image output (e.g., one specific dog face) that consistently fools the discriminator, and repeatedly generates only that dog—losing all dataset diversity! Wasserstein GANs (WGAN) and Diffusion models eliminate mode collapse.
  • Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) in Diffusion: Controls how strictly a Diffusion model adheres to a user's text prompt. It interpolates between unconditional noise predictions $\mathbf{\epsilon}_{u}$ and text-conditioned predictions $\mathbf{\epsilon}_{c}$:
    • \[ \mathbf{\epsilon}_{\text{guided}} = \mathbf{\epsilon}_{u} + w \cdot \left( \mathbf{\epsilon}_{c} - \mathbf{\epsilon}_{u} \right) \] Higher scale $w \in [7, 12]$ forces strict prompt alignment, while lower $w$ increases creative diversity!

⚡ Practical Decision Guide: Generative Model Paradigms

Paradigm Generation Quality Training Stability Sampling Speed When to Use
Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) Moderate (Tend to be slightly blurry) High (Stable ELBO loss) Fast 1-step decoder forward pass Smooth latent space interpolation, fast generation, feature representation learning
Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) High (Sharp crisp details) Unstable (Minimax mode collapse risk) Fast 1-step generator pass Real-time video synthesis, fast image-to-image translation, deepfake detectors
Diffusion Models (DDPM / Latent) State-of-the-Art (Highest detail) High (Stable U-Net MSE score loss) Slow (Iterative 20-50 denoising steps) Highest-quality text-to-image synthesis (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion), audio synthesis
