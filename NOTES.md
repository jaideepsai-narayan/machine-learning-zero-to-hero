# User Preferences & Working Notes

## Learner Profile
- **Background**: Zero prior AI/ML experience. Complete beginner ("dummies/noobs" perspective).
- **Learning Style**: Visual, playful, interactive, pictorial. Wants diagrams and interactive widgets.
- **Math Tolerance**: Wants clear mathematical derivations, BUT broken down step-by-step with zero skipped steps, color-coded variables, and real-world analogies.
- **Pacing**: Micro-sessions (chunking big concepts into bite-sized sub-lessons).

## Teaching Strategy
- Every lesson starts with an intuitive story or real-world mystery.
- Every formula has an interactive diagram or slider showing what happens when numbers change.
- Quiz questions use identical choice lengths and provide instant visual feedback.
- Build up from simplest ideas (predicting house prices with a straight line) to complex ideas (neural nets & backprop).
