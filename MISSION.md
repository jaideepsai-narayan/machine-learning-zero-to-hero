# Mission: Machine Learning from Zero to Hero (For Complete Beginners)

## Why
Master machine learning from absolute scratch—with zero prior AI background—by building playful, intuitive mental models, visual diagrams, and friendly step-by-step mathematical derivations.

## Success looks like
- Intuitively explain core ML concepts (Supervised/Unsupervised, Regressors, Classifiers, Loss, Gradient Descent, Neural Nets) to anyone.
- Comfortably read and derive key ML math formulas step-by-step without fear of notation.
- Build interactive ML algorithms in Python/NumPy from first principles.

## Constraints
- **Zero prior AI assumption**: Every term, variable, and concept must be introduced with a visual analogy first.
- **Bite-sized chunking**: Big concepts must be split into short, digestible 5-10 minute micro-lessons.
- **Playful & Pictorial**: Every lesson must feature diagrams, SVG/Canvas interactive widgets, and colorful equations.

## Out of scope
- Black-box magic (using complex libraries like PyTorch/Scikit-learn before understanding the math under the hood).
- Dense textbook wall-of-text explanations without diagrams or interactive feedback.
